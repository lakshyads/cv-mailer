# Interview Preparation Assistant

**Priority**: Nice-to-Have  
**Status**: Not Started (Future)  
**Phase**: Future  
**Estimated Timeline**: TBD

---

## Description

Help prepare for interviews with company research, common questions, experience mapping, and mock interview preparation. Provides comprehensive interview preparation tools linked to job applications.

**Key Goals**:
- Company research summary
- Common interview questions
- Relevant experience mapping
- Mock interview questions
- Notes and reminders

---

## Use Cases

1. **Interview Prep**: Prepare for upcoming interviews
2. **Company Research**: Get company information and insights
3. **Question Preparation**: Practice common interview questions
4. **Experience Mapping**: Map your experience to job requirements
5. **Interview Notes**: Store interview notes and feedback

---

- Industry reports

- Recent news/developments
- Products/services

- Industry position

- Web scraping or API integration
- AI-powered research summary (optional)

- General interview questions

### Relevant Experience Mapping

#### Experience Analysis

- Map your experience to job requirements

- Prepare talking points

- Analyze job description requirements
- Match with resume/experience

### Mock Interview Questions

- Practice scenarios

#### Mock Interview Mode
